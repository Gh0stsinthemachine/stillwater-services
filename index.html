What we do

Services for every water garden

Seasonal · Spring

Spring Clean-Out

Your pond wakes up healthy after winter — fish cared for, water treated, everything reset.

  • Removing any winter netting
  • Pumping out and replacing water
  • Catching and safely storing fish during the process
  • Removing muck, debris, and accumulated leaves
  • Cleaning out skimmer, waterfall tanks, and filters
  • Power washing rocks if needed
  • Adding treatments — algaecide, dechlorinator, beneficial bacteria, and water clarifier
Ongoing · Monthly

Monthly Maintenance

Your pond stays beautiful all season. You don't have to think about it at all.

  • Scrubbing and cleaning the waterfall
  • Clearing algae and duckweed
  • Rinsing filters
  • Feeding plants
  • Adding water treatments as needed
  • On-call to address any concerns between visits
Seasonal · Fall

Fall Winterization

Your pond protected through the Ohio winter — so you start spring in great shape.

  • Everything included in monthly maintenance
  • Heater placement if needed for your fish
  • Protective netting to keep out debris through winter
